the little cat walks through the garden.
the little dog waits by the gate.
the cat sees the dog, and the dog sees the cat.
they wait, they watch, and they walk.

the morning is quiet.
the wind moves through the trees.
the leaves move, the grass moves, and the birds sing.
the cat walks under the tree.
the dog walks near the wall.

where is the bird?
the bird is in the tree.
where is the cat?
the cat is in the garden.
where is the dog?
the dog is by the gate.

the baker opens the shop.
the baker makes bread.
the people walk to the shop, and the people buy bread.
the baker says, 'good morning!'
the people say, 'good morning!'

the day grows warm.
the cat sleeps in the sun.
the dog sleeps by the wall.
the bird sings in the tree.

later, the wind grows strong.
the leaves move again.
the cat wakes, the dog wakes, and the people walk home.
the street becomes quiet.

the little cat walks through the garden again.
the little dog waits by the gate again.
the bird sings, the wind moves, and the day becomes night.
